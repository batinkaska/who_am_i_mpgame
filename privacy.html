Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026

Who Am I? Game ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app and website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to operate the game:

  • Player Name: The nickname you enter to join a game room. This is visible to other players in the same room.
  • Character Names: The famous character names you and other players enter during gameplay.
  • Anonymous Player ID: A randomly generated identifier stored locally on your device to maintain your session. This ID is not linked to your real identity.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data collected is used solely to:

  • Enable real-time multiplayer gameplay
  • Maintain your game session
  • Display player names and game history within the room

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or any purpose beyond operating the game.

3. Data Storage

Game room data is stored temporarily on Firebase Realtime Database (Google) and is automatically deleted after the game ends. Your anonymous player ID is stored locally on your device only.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Firebase Realtime Database by Google to power real-time multiplayer functionality. Google's privacy policy applies to this service: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only data collected is the nickname and character names entered during gameplay, which are not linked to any real identity.

6. Data Retention

Game room data is deleted automatically after gameplay. Your local anonymous ID remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personally identifiable information, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. If you have concerns, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
